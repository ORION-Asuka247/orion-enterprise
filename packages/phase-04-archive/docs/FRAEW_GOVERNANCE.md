# FRAEW Reporting Governance

ORION uses the ASUKA247 F.R.A.E.W structure where appropriate:

- Fault - the identified defect or condition
- Risk - consequence and compliance/safety significance
- Action - proportionate remedial action
- Evidence - supporting inspection evidence
- Who - responsible party or role

FRAEW is a reporting structure, not itself a statutory standard.

Every regulatory assertion within a FRAEW report should remain traceable to the approved ORION rule/source record.
